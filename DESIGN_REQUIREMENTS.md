## Application

- Use the referenced visual language as the baseline for color, typography,
  spacing, component styling, elevation, responsive behavior, and interaction
  states.
- Apply the system to a medication-reminder product rather than reproducing an
  Apple storefront. Favor clear schedules, medication status, reminders, and
  repeated daily actions over marketing-style hero composition.
- Preserve strong hierarchy, generous whitespace, restrained surfaces, clear
  focus states, and minimum 44px touch targets.
- Product requirements, patient safety, accessibility, legibility, and platform
  conventions take precedence over purely decorative guidance.
- When an implementation intentionally departs from the reference, document the
  reason in the relevant design or code review.
